Research & projects

Three themes, one program

My research connects individual animals to ecosystem-scale change. Large-scale models reveal the patterns, individual-based models recover the mechanisms behind them, and together they support applied decisions.

Theme I · Pattern

Quantitative ecosystem & population dynamics

Large-scale Ecopath with Ecosim and Ecospace models that quantify how disturbance, harvest, and climate reshape marine food webs across decades and regions.

Oceanic Gulf of Mexico after Deepwater Horizon

An ecosystem model of the deep-pelagic Gulf built to estimate mortality following the 2010 oil spill and forecast the food web's response. About one quarter of trophic interactions shifted in strength from 2011 to 2018, an early warning of indirect change.

Woodstock et al. (2021) Ecological Modelling · Woodstock & Zhang (2022) Deep-Sea Research I

Marine mammals, seabirds & fisheries in the North Sea

An ecosystem model comparing the predatory impact of recovering marine mammals and seabirds with the exploitative impact of industrial fisheries. Population changes in top predators had limited impact on fisheries catches, supporting an ecosystem-based view of apparent competition.

Woodstock et al. (2025) Canadian Journal of Fisheries and Aquatic Sciences

Gulf-wide continental shelf & reef fish under climate change

A U.S. Gulf-wide Ecopath with Ecosim and Ecospace model incorporating spatial-temporal environmental drivers, used to identify the reef fish "winners" and "losers" of a changing ocean and to separate environmental from fishing effects.

Harris, Sagarese, Woodstock & Chagaris (2026) NOAA Tech. Memo. NMFS-SEFSC-797
Theme II · Mechanism

Advanced computational technologies

Individual-based and agent-based models that let population- and ecosystem-scale properties emerge from the behavior and physiology of individual animals.

Open source

SwimmingIndividuals

A Julia agent-based ecosystem model in which each animal moves through 3D space under temperature-dependent bioenergetics, with size- and distance-based predation so diets and functional responses emerge rather than being prescribed. Runs large simulations on hybrid GPU–CPU hardware.

Woodstock, Mattern, Wu & Britten (in review) PLOS Computational Biology
Co-developer

Valkyrie

A cloud-based agent-based model for fisheries assessment, built with NOAA's Office of Science and Technology, that integrates oceanographic and trait databases to predict fish bioenergetics, movement, and survival with an accessible visual interface.

With NOAA Office of Science and Technology

Carbon export by mesopelagic fishes

A trait-based, individual-scale model of active carbon transport. In the oceanic Gulf of Mexico, the mesopelagic community transports 11 to 24% of dissolved organic carbon each year, an effort led by lanternfishes.

Woodstock, Sutton & Zhang (2022) Limnology and Oceanography

Nitrogen transport by oceanic cetaceans

A spatially explicit, individual-based model of the "whale pump." Most transported nitrogen originates in the upper mesopelagic and adds an estimated 8 to 16% to surface ammonium, a meaningful contribution in a nutrient-limited system.

Woodstock et al. (2023) Limnology and Oceanography
Theme III · Application

Seascape dynamics, habitat & applied management

Coupling ecological understanding with economics and management to inform stock assessment, restoration, and spatial planning.

NOAA SEFSC

King Mackerel ecosystem-based assessment

Bringing ecosystem context into a single-species stock assessment, evaluating environmental and trophic drivers of Gulf of America King Mackerel to inform the SEDAR 99 assessment.

Woodstock & Sagarese (2026) SEDAR 99-WP-05
PI

Deep-Sea Benefits: open-ocean restoration

Quantifying the ecological outcomes of mesophotic and deep benthic community restoration in the Gulf, funded by the Deepwater Horizon Open Ocean Trustee Implementation Group.

Deepwater Horizon Trustee Council · 2026–2028
PI

Artificial structures: installation & decommissioning

Leading a multi-institutional NCEAS working group assessing how marine artificial structures reshape reef fish communities, coordinating systematic mapping, time-series, and ecosystem-modeling subgroups to inform where structures are placed and removed.

NCEAS Gulf Ecosystem Initiative · 2026–2027
Lead modeler

FRESCA: Southwest Florida under climate change

A high-resolution Ecospace model for the Florida Keys and Florida Bay that integrates downscaled CMIP6 climate projections to predict habitat persistence and species distribution shifts, and tests whether restoration can offset climate effects.

Florida Regional Ecosystem Stressors Collaborative Assessment

Living-habitat restoration bioeconomics, Chesapeake Bay

Coupling Ecopath with Ecosim models to an IMPLAN economic model to estimate how oyster restoration and seagrass change affect the regional commercial fishing economy, showing that oyster and vegetation habitats must be managed together.

Woodstock et al. (2024) Ecological Modelling
